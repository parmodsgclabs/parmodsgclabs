About Us

Craftsmanship in Glass, Backed by Science

SGC Labs Glass is a manufacturer and supplier of laboratory glassware and scientific glass apparatus. We combine skilled glassblowing craftsmanship with modern production and quality control to serve laboratories, industries and institutions — both domestically and across international markets.

🏭 In-House Manufacturing

Complete control over raw material selection, fabrication, annealing and calibration for consistent quality in every batch.

🧰 Custom Fabrication

Bespoke glass apparatus built to your drawings and specifications — distillation assemblies, reactors, and specialty joints.

🚚 Reliable Supply

Dependable lead times, secure export packaging and responsive support for bulk, OEM and dealer orders.

Laboratory Glassware Range

Beakers & Flasks

Griffin beakers, Erlenmeyer (conical) flasks, volumetric, round-bottom and flat-bottom flasks in all standard capacities.

Volumetric Glassware

Condensers & Distillation
